What it holds

The database is a single SQLite file holding the records behind the index: the legal instruments in each jurisdiction and their official texts, the provisions each De Jure score rests on, every score in the index, De Jure and De Facto, with its factors, rationale or evidence, and the sources cited for the De Facto scores.

Scores are stored one per row, in assessments. Dimension scores, indices and Effective Contestability are not stored: the views v_dimension_scores, v_index and v_effective compute them from assessments whenever they are read.

Each table below opens in this page with Show rows: search it, filter it, sort it by any column, click a row to see all of its fields, and download the matching rows as a CSV file. The first time, the page loads the database file (10.5 MB). The same file can be downloaded and opened with any SQLite tool, such as DB Browser for SQLite or the sqlite3 command line.

Scores

Every score assessments

table · 320 rows · 29 columns

Every score in the index, one row per score, in three sets: De Jure at jurisdiction level (16 mechanisms in 9 jurisdictions, June 2026; 144 rows); De Jure at market level (Google Search and the Apple App Store in December 2023 and June 2026, and Google Play in June 2026; 80 rows); and De Facto (Google Search, the Apple App Store and Google Play in December 2023 and July 2026; 96 rows). A De Jure score is the minimum of its three factor scores. Each row is scored, missing (Applicable but Missing) or not_applicable; only scored rows have a score.

Effective Contestability v_effective

view · 6 rows · 11 columns

Effective Contestability for each gatekeeper–market pair and period, computed from the market-level De Jure and De Facto indices under four formulas: the product (ec_multiplicative), the arithmetic mean with λ = 0.5 (ec_arithmetic), the minimum (ec_bottleneck) and the implementation gap, De Jure minus De Facto (implementation_gap). Google Play has no December 2023 De Jure record, so its baseline row has no Effective value.

Columns (11)
ColumnTypeKeyRefers toNote

periodtext
de_jure_observedtext
de_facto_observedtext
de_jure—market-level De Jure index
de_facto—De Facto index
ec_multiplicative—De Jure × De Facto
ec_arithmetic—0.5 × De Jure + 0.5 × De Facto
ec_bottleneck—the smaller of the two
implementation_gap—De Jure − De Facto
