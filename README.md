# Online appendix: Measuring Contestability in Digital Markets

Online appendix to *Beyond Market Concentration: Measuring Contestability in Digital Markets* (Zhiyang Cheng, Tracy Xu, Amr Yakout).

Page: https://zycheng114514.github.io/contestability-appendix/

The page gives the content of the *Contestability Index Coding Handbook* (V4.1, July 2026) in three parts:

| Part | Handbook sections |
| --- | --- |
| Summary | I; the aggregate rows of the tables in IV.A, V.A.3 and V.B.3; V.A.4 and V.B.4 (Effective Contestability) |
| De Jure Scoring | II; IV; V.A.2 and V.B.2 (market-level De Jure) |
| De Facto Scoring | III; the opening text of V, V.A and V.B; V.A.1, V.A.3, V.B.1 and V.B.3 |

Section numbers, text and tables are as in the Handbook. `index.html` is generated from the Handbook file, so corrections belong in the Handbook, not in this repository.

The page is kept out of search engines (`noindex`), but anyone with the link can open it.
